Pure Client-Side Architecture

Engineered for reliable, high-fidelity document speech

No backend servers, no cloud fees, and no third-party telemetry. LEXIO executes all file parsing and speech synthesis inside your device's browser thread.

Native Web Speech Synthesis

Utilizes your operating system's built-in voice synthesizer (macOS, Windows, iOS, Android, Linux) with zero API costs, zero network lag, and full offline capability.

Synchronized Visual Tracking

Reads by sentence and clause rather than robotic single-word steps. The document viewer tracks active phrases in real-time with adjustable automatic centering.

Multilingual & Accent Filtering

Filters available voices by language (English, Hindi, Spanish, Italian, Arabic), regional accent, and persona with speed and pitch fine-tuning.

In-Browser Document Extraction

Extracts text from complex PDFs, Word documents, PowerPoint presentations, Excel spreadsheets, and EPUB books locally without sending a byte over the internet.

How LEXIO Works in 3 Clear Steps

No account verification emails, no complex configuration. Get to reading in seconds.

1

Import or Paste Content

Drop any supported document onto the workspace or paste text directly into the editable source panel.

2

Select Voice & Pacing

Choose your preferred language, accent, and persona. Adjust reading speed and pitch to match your natural listening cadence.

3

Listen with Synchronized Focus

Private by Design: Zero Server Drama

LEXIO is a 100% pure client web application. It requires no Node.js runtime in production, connects to no backend analytics, and transmits no document contents to remote servers. All your notes, accounts, and audio settings reside entirely in your browser's private local storage.

User Manual

1. Choose a Workspace View

Use Dashboard for executive summaries, Reader for synchronized playback, and AI Studio for glossary and structure review.

2. Import or Paste Content

Open Reader, upload a supported document or paste raw text. LEXIO will parse it into a clean, phrase-tracked reading surface.

3. Tune Your Voice

Select your language, accent, and persona. You can adjust speed and pitch using the smooth range sliders.

4. Intelligent Output Commands

Switch reading modes anytime between Executive Brief, Study Notes, Presentation Flow, and Accessibility mode.

5. Synchronized Audio Playback

Click Start. Visual highlights follow the spoken sentence and automatically center the reading surface.
